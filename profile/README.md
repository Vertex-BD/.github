## Four Beats

1. **Name the job.** Give the Vertex BD residential framing a short title before you place studs.
2. **Pin the live walls.** Keep current assemblies on the Vertex BD wood framing model and leave unused bays aside.
3. **Read the panels.** Open Vertex BD panel drawings before you move a stud that already has a sheet.
4. **Check the assembly.** Read the Vertex BD wall assembly before you archive a panel.
5. **Close the loop.** Clear finished panels so the next Vertex BD visit does not start with a search.

## First Questions

**Does Vertex BD replace every other program?**
No. Vertex BD sits beside the panel saw list, browser, and files you already use.

**Can two people share one computer?**
Yes. Each person keeps a separate Vertex BD profile.

**Will Vertex BD feel heavy on an older laptop?**
No. Vertex BD is meant to stay readable during a normal framing session.

**What should a new user try first?**
One small Vertex BD wall assembly today, then the same panel drawings tomorrow.

**Do panel drawings leave the stud model?**
No. Vertex BD panel drawings stay with the Vertex BD wall assembly on the same residential framing set.

**Is Vertex BD only for a full house?**
No. Vertex BD can hold a single named panel job while the rest of the office stays off the board.

## Field Note

Vertex BD is easier to trust when the residential framing set stays smaller than the folder it came from. A named job shows what is active, the panel pins show what you will issue today, and the closing note tells the next person which wall assembly changed.

The same loop fits a single garage wall, a panel run, or an office shared by two people. Open the last Vertex BD wood framing model, finish one panel drawing, and leave fewer mixed 2D sheets than you found.

A review should not have to guess which stud model belongs to the panel sheet. Keep that pair together inside Vertex BD.

## One Real Pass

Pick a panelized wall that already has a name. Open it in Vertex BD, keep only those studs on the framing model, and finish one panel drawing you can see.
